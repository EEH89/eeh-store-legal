This policy explains how EEH Store ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you interact with our store through WhatsApp.

How we use your information

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
